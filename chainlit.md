# Welcome to FitMind 💪

Your personal AI fitness trainer that remembers everything.

## What I can do

- **Coach your sessions** — tell me your workout plan and I'll give you progressive overload targets
- **Track nutrition** — log your meals and I'll track your macros
- **Weekly planning** — ask me to plan your week based on your history
- **Progress reports** — ask how you're doing and I'll analyse your trends

## How to use

Just talk to me naturally:
- *"Pull day today, planning lat pulldown and rows"*
- *"Had chicken rice for lunch"*  
- *"Plan my week"*
- *"How am I doing?"*
